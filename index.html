Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

The Ritual Barbershop ("we", "us") operates this website and the barbershop at Khreshchatyk st., 25, Kyiv. Contact for privacy questions: ritual@barbershop.ua.

2. What data we collect

When you book an appointment, request a consultation or order a product, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, email address, the chosen date and time, the service or product, and any comment you add. We do not ask for payment card details on this website.

3. Why we use it

We use your data only to confirm and manage your appointment, call you about a consultation or order, remind you about your visit and answer your questions. We do not use it for any other purpose without your consent.

4. Legal basis

We process your data because you have asked us for a service and given your consent by submitting the form. You can withdraw consent at any time by writing to ritual@barbershop.ua.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. It may be seen only by our staff and by service providers that help us run the website and bookings, and only as far as needed. We may disclose data if the law requires it.

6. Storage

We keep your data for as long as needed to provide the service and for up to 24 months after your last visit, unless the law requires a longer period. After that it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You may ask us to show you what data we hold, correct it, delete it or limit its use. Send a request to ritual@barbershop.ua and we will reply within 30 days.

8. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser may store technical data needed for the site to work.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version and its date are always shown on this page.

User Agreement

1. General

By using this website and booking through it you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

2. Booking

An appointment is confirmed after we contact you by phone or email. Choose a date and time that suits you; we may offer another slot if yours is no longer available.

3. Prices

Prices shown on the website are starting prices ("from"). The final price depends on hair length, complexity and extra care, and is agreed with your barber before the work begins and does not change during the process.

4. Cancellation and lateness

Please let us know at least 3 hours before your visit if you need to cancel or move it. If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may shorten the service or move your appointment to the next free time.

5. Products

Products and tools shown in the shop block are ordered through the request form. We confirm availability, price and delivery or pick-up by phone before the order is final.

6. Your information

You promise that the details you submit are correct. How we handle them is described in the Privacy Policy.

7. Content

Texts, photos, logo and design of this website belong to The Ritual Barbershop or are used with permission. Do not copy or reuse them without our written consent.

8. Liability

We do our best to keep the website accurate and available but cannot guarantee it will always be free of errors or interruptions. Our liability is limited to the extent allowed by law.
